# SoloDeckBench FARS-style

Run:

```bash
python -m solodeck_v3.bench.benchmark_runner
```

Benchmark tasks:

- 10 ideation
- 10 planning
- 10 experiment
- 10 writing
- 10 causal validity
- 10 failure repair

Metrics include route accuracy, artifact validity, statistical warning recall, causal overclaim rate, repair success and reward variance.
